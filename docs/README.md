# OctoAcme Project Management

This documentation describes OctoAcme’s customer-first, iterative approach to delivering product features, services, and integrations. Projects move from validating an idea through planning, delivery, release, and reflection, with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and shared learning throughout.

## Project lifecycle

1. **Initiation** — Confirm the business need, outcomes, stakeholders, initial risks, and team availability. A reviewed one-pager and go/no-go decision authorize planning.
2. **Planning** — Turn the approved initiative into a prioritized backlog, estimates, acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, and a test approach. Surface dependencies and risks early.
3. **Execution & tracking** — Deliver and review small increments using the project board, regular team syncs, and demos. Track progress and outcomes, keep the risk register current, and escalate blockers as needed. Quality is built in through automated tests, code review, CI security scans, and manual QA when needed.
4. **Release & deployment** — Confirm acceptance criteria and CI/security checks pass, prepare release notes and a rollback plan, deploy, and verify the release. Communicate the release to stakeholders and support.
5. **Retrospective & continuous improvement** — After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, capture learnings and assign a small number of actionable improvements with owners and due dates.

**Risk management and communication span the lifecycle:** identify and assess risks early, track mitigations and dependencies, review status regularly, and escalate from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed. Use weekly PM/Product alignment, delivery syncs, and weekly or milestone-based stakeholder updates to keep decisions and progress visible.

## Core roles

- **Project Manager (PM):** Coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications.
- **Product Manager (PdM):** Defines outcomes, prioritizes the roadmap and backlog, and measures success.
- **Developers:** Estimate, design, implement, test, and review work while identifying technical risks.
- **QA/Testing:** Validates quality and acceptance criteria through planned testing and manual QA when needed.
- **Stakeholders:** Provide input, alignment, and approvals.
